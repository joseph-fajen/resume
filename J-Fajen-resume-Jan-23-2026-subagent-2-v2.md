### When Documentation Needed AI — Essential Cardano Assistant

**The Challenge:** The Cardano ecosystem spans thousands of documents across dozens of sources. Users needed instant, accurate answers — but any AI assistant in this space risked hallucination, which in blockchain could mean real financial consequences.

**What I Did:** I built a production AI chatbot end-to-end, solo. I ingested 2,500 documents from 8 sources, built a complete extraction pipeline, and iterated through 20+ system prompt versions until achieving zero detectable hallucination and 100% fact accuracy across targeted test queries. I deployed a professional Next.js web UI with streaming and conversation persistence, established Railway production infrastructure, and resolved critical blockers (API limits, domain migrations, package conflicts) independently — 91 commits from concept to production.

**The Result:** A production-ready AI assistant that provides accurate, trustworthy answers to complex blockchain questions. Test deployment: https://powerful-motivation-production.up.railway.app/
