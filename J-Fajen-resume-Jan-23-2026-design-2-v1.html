When Documentation Needed AI

Essential Cardano Assistant

The Challenge: The Cardano ecosystem spans thousands of documents across dozens of sources. Users needed instant, accurate answers -- but any AI assistant in this space risked hallucination, which in blockchain could mean real financial consequences.

What I Did: I built a production AI chatbot end-to-end, solo. I ingested 2,500 documents from 8 sources, built a complete extraction pipeline, and iterated through 20+ system prompt versions until achieving zero detectable hallucination and 100% fact accuracy across targeted test queries. Deployed with Next.js, Railway infrastructure -- 91 commits from concept to production.

The Result: A production-ready AI assistant that provides accurate, trustworthy answers to complex blockchain questions.
